Floating Mute puts a small, movable mute button on top of every app. Watching YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok or any video where you want to silence the sound instantly? One tap does it, another tap brings the sound back.

<b>Features</b>
- One-tap mute and unmute of your media volume.
- Unmuting restores the exact volume you had before.
- The button icon follows the current volume level (off, 25%, 50%, 75%, max).
- Optional volume percentage label next to the button.
- Drag it anywhere on the screen. Its position is remembered separately for portrait and landscape.
- Drag it to the bottom edge to tuck it away, like a chat bubble. Bring it back from the notification.
- Customise the button: size, background and icon colours, opacity, corner radius, and label colours.
- Light, Dark or System app theme, with Material You colours on Android 12 and newer.
- Works on old phones too: Android 5.0 (API 21) and newer.
- No ads, no tracking, no internet permission. Free software under GPLv3.

<b>How to use</b>
- Tap the button to mute or unmute.
- Hold briefly, then drag to move it.
- Drag it to the bottom edge and release to hide it.
- Long press the button to show or hide the volume percentage.
- Use the notification to Hide, Show or Stop the button at any time.
- Tap the notification to open the settings and change the look.

<b>Permissions</b>
- Display over other apps: needed to draw the button on top of other apps.
- Notifications: used for the Hide, Show and Stop controls.
- Modify audio settings: needed to change the media volume.
- Ignore battery optimisations (optional): keeps the button alive on some phones.

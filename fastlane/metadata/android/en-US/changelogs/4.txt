- Fixed a crash on launch on Android 14+ (a broadcast receiver was registered without an export flag).
- The floating button now runs as a proper foreground service, so Android no longer kills it in the background.
- Fully redesigned, modern Material 3 interface with Material You colour support, light/dark themes and a card-based layout.
- New "Themes" screen: customise the button's size, colours, opacity and corner radius with a live preview; choose System / Light / Dark app theme.
- Hide the button by dragging it to the bottom edge of the screen (like chat bubbles) instead of long-pressing.
- Long-press now toggles the volume-percentage label.
- A short hold is required before a drag starts, so quick mute/unmute taps are no longer swallowed by movement.
- Fixed settings being reset and the button failing to hide from the notification.
- Smoother dragging: the position is no longer written to storage on every frame.
